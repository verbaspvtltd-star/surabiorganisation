Our Story · Since 2013

Rooted in trade. Growing with every link in the supply chain.

From industrial raw-material supply to feed distribution, and from agricultural sourcing to retail and wholesale markets, our journey has been shaped by one consistent purpose: connecting businesses and customers with the products they need.

Celebrating Farmers and You
Who We Are

A business built through experience, relationships and opportunity

Surabi Organisation Private Limited is a growing supply, distribution and market-focused organisation with roots dating back to 2013. Over the years, we have developed our business across different product categories by staying close to customers, understanding market needs and adapting to the realities of sourcing and distribution.

Our journey began with the supply of coconut shell powder and palm nut shell to various industries. This early experience gave us a practical understanding of industrial requirements, supplier relationships, product movement and the importance of dependable fulfilment.

As our experience grew, we expanded into fish and prawn feed distribution in 2017. Working in this segment brought us closer to the needs of the aquaculture sector and strengthened our understanding of distribution networks, product availability and timely supply.

Over time, we also recognised recurring challenges in agricultural supply chains—particularly the availability, sourcing and movement of fruits and exotic produce. These experiences helped shape our next step: building a stronger connection between producers, suppliers, wholesale markets and retail customers.

Our Journey

More than a decade of learning and expansion

Each stage of our journey has added new experience, capabilities and a clearer understanding of the markets we serve.

2013

Our beginnings in industrial supply

Surabi began by supplying coconut shell powder and palm nut shell to various industries. This laid the foundation for our business through supplier coordination, product sourcing and serving industrial customers.

2017

Expansion into aquaculture feed distribution

We expanded into the distribution of fish and prawn feed, broadening our market reach and building experience in serving the requirements of the aquaculture industry.

A new direction

Recognising agricultural supply-chain gaps

Our market experience highlighted challenges around the sourcing, availability and movement of agricultural produce, especially fruits and exotic varieties. We saw an opportunity to contribute through more connected sourcing and distribution.

2021

Entering retail and wholesale markets

We started retail stores and wholesale supply operations, procuring products from different parts of the country. This step brought us closer to market demand and helped us build wider sourcing connections for agricultural produce.

Today

Building stronger connections from source to market

We continue to build on our experience across sourcing, distribution and supply-chain coordination, with a focus on dependable relationships, product availability and serving evolving customer requirements.

What Guides Us

Our approach to serving the market

We believe that a dependable supply business is built on more than moving products. It requires understanding the people, processes and expectations at every stage.

Strong sourcing connections

We work to connect with suppliers and procurement channels across different regions so we can respond to a wider range of market requirements.

Market understanding

Our experience across industrial supply, aquaculture feed and agricultural produce helps us appreciate that every product category has its own sourcing and distribution needs.

Availability-focused service

We understand how product availability affects businesses and customers. We aim to coordinate sourcing and supply with care and clear communication.

Long-term relationships

We value the trust of suppliers, business partners, retailers, wholesale customers and the communities connected to the products we serve.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a trusted and well-connected supply and distribution organisation that helps bridge the gap between producers, suppliers, markets and customers—making quality products more accessible through responsible sourcing and dependable service.

Our Mission

To understand changing market needs, develop reliable sourcing relationships and coordinate the supply of products with care, transparency and consistency. We aim to grow through customer focus, operational learning and long-term partnerships.
